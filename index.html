STEP 1: BARE DIRT PREPARATION
1

Bare Dirt Grading, Clearing & Tamping

Since there is no grass on the path site, focus directly on clearing debris and compacting the bare earth. Rake away sticks, sharp rocks, exposed roots, and organic matter across the entire 150-foot length.

Dampen Soil: Lightly mist bare dirt with a garden hose before tamping. Dry soil crumbles, whereas slightly moist soil compacts into a rock-hard base.
Compact Thoroughly: Use a 10" hand tamper or rent a mechanical plate compactor. Pack down high spots and fill low spots until walking on the bare soil leaves no footprint.
STEP 2: NON-WOVEN FABRIC & PINS
2

Heavy Non-Woven Geotextile Rollout

Unroll heavy-duty non-woven geotextile fabric (4oz–6oz weight) directly over the compacted bare earth. Non-woven fabric allows water to drain straight into the ground while preventing mud from pumping up into your gravel.

Pro-Tip: Overlap fabric seams by at least 8 to 12 inches. Drive 6-inch galvanized sod staple pins every 3 feet along edges and seams to keep fabric tight while working.
STEP 3: BRICK BORDER & SPLIT GRID STRIPS
3

Brick Borders & Solution A (Long-Cut Grid Strips)

Lay red clay/concrete bricks end-to-end along both sides of your 3-foot pathway boundary over the fabric. Drive 10-inch steel timber spikes through every brick into the earth.

Solution A Grid Installation (No Box Cutting!):

Unroll your 36-inch wide geocell roll. Using a utility knife, make one single continuous longitudinal cut right down the length of the roll to split it into two ~10-inch wide strips.

Place Strip A against the left brick line and Strip B against the right brick line. This automatically leaves an open 16-inch middle channel for your stepping pavers!

STEP 4: CENTER CHANNEL SAND & PAVERS
4

Center Channel Sand Bedding & Paver Setting

In the open 16-inch middle channel between the two grid strips, lay a 0.5-inch layer of coarse levelling sand where each paver will sit.

Set your 16"×16" concrete stepping pavers on the sand pads spaced 24 inches apart. Tap with a rubber mallet until the top surface of each paver is 100% flush with the top edge of the 2-inch side grid strips and brick border.

STEP 5: GRAVEL FILL & RAKING
5

Pea Gravel Pouring & Geocell Filling

Pour clean, dry 3/8" pea gravel into the left and right geocell grid strips and fill in the spaces around the stepping pavers. Rake the gravel flat so it sits exactly level with the tops of the grid walls and pavers.

Important: Lightly tamp or walk over the gravel to settle the pebbles deep into the honeycomb cells before applying glue binder.
STEP 6: ROCK GLUE & 48HR CURE
6

Pet-Safe Rock Glue Application & Paw Quarantine

Using a garden pump sprayer or pre-mixing in a wheelbarrow, apply pet-safe permeable rock binder glue (such as Easihold or SRW) in 2 to 3 heavy saturating coats over the gravel.

Mandatory 48-Hour Dog Quarantine: Keep your Standard Poodle completely off the path for 24 to 48 hours while the glue fully cures in warm, dry weather. High-speed running before curing will break the bonded top crust.
